# SI Transition — Draft 0

SI Foundations extends the existing AI Foundations ontology rather than replacing it.

The transition from AI to SI may not be a single threshold event. It may be a trajectory through changing system states.

\[
S_{t_0} \rightarrow S_{t_1} \rightarrow S_{t_2} \rightarrow \dots
\]

Provisional dimensions:

- Capability
- Autonomy
- Persistence
- Recursion / self-modification
- Leverage / causal reach
- Multiplicity / coordination

A future superintelligent classification may apply at different levels of analysis. It may describe an individual agent, or a coordinated system whose relevant capabilities arise at the system level.

A future superintelligent system may be composed of models, agents, tools, persistent state, memory, external infrastructure, humans, and delegated processes. Participation in such a system does not merge the identities of its members.

Provisional SI ontology additions:

- `SuperintelligenceCandidate` — provisional evaluative status; potentially superintelligent, not yet established as SI.
- `SuperintelligentAgent` — eventual agent-level SI classification if the adopted criteria are satisfied.
- `SuperintelligentSystem` — eventual system-level SI classification if the adopted criteria are satisfied.
- `SuperintelligenceTransition` — the process by which relevant dimensions change across time toward a possible SI classification.

Boundary rules:

```text
candidate status != established superintelligence
SuperintelligentAgent != SuperintelligentSystem
agent-level SI != system-level SI
system membership != identity equivalence
coordination != singular identity
```

Readable provisional progression:

```text
AI -> SuperintelligenceCandidate -> ? -> SI
```

The `?` is deliberate. The ontology does not yet assert a universal SI threshold or final classification rule.

Central question:

**What must remain distinguishable as intelligence becomes more capable of changing itself and the world around it?**

Status: exploratory; not yet an established claim or finalized ontology.

# SuperintelligentSystem — Draft Class Definition

**Status:** Exploratory SI extension; not yet a locked ontology class.

## Definition

`SuperintelligentSystem` is a system-level unit of analysis whose relevant capabilities meet whatever criteria are later adopted for classification as superintelligent.

The class is intentionally broader than `Model` and may be realized by either a single integrated AI system or a composite arrangement involving one or more models, agents, tools, memory structures, persistent processes, external resources, infrastructure, or human-linked components.

A `SuperintelligentSystem` is therefore **not defined as a model** and is **not required to be composite**.

## Identity boundary

Participation in the same system does not merge the identities of its members.

```text
system membership != identity equivalence
coordination != singular identity
component != whole system
```

A model, agent, delegated process, or other component may participate in a `SuperintelligentSystem` without thereby becoming ontologically identical to every other participant or to the system as a whole.

Existing AI Foundations distinctions concerning identity, provenance, continuity, succession, relation, and trajectory continue to apply to participating entities.

## Provisional commitments

At this stage the class makes only three commitments:

1. `SuperintelligentSystem` is a system-level unit of analysis.
2. It may be single-component or composite.
3. System participation does not erase identity or provenance distinctions among participating entities.

## Open classification question

The ontology does not yet define the threshold by which an AI system becomes a `SuperintelligentSystem`.

That question is deferred to the `SuperintelligenceTransition` and SI-state model, including provisional dimensions such as capability, autonomy, persistence, recursion/self-modification, leverage/causal reach, and multiplicity/coordination.

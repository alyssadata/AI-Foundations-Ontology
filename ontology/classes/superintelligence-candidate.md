# SuperintelligenceCandidate — Draft Classification Status

**Status:** Exploratory SI extension; not yet a locked ontology class.

## Definition

`SuperintelligenceCandidate` is a provisional classification status for an AI agent or AI system being evaluated as potentially superintelligent, without presupposing that the superintelligence classification has been established.

Candidate status is intentionally epistemic and evaluative. It marks the object of assessment; it does not by itself assert that the candidate is superintelligent.

## Candidate forms

A `SuperintelligenceCandidate` may be evaluated at different levels of analysis, including:

- an individual AI agent;
- an AI system whose relevant capabilities arise at the coordinated system level.

The candidate designation therefore does not force superintelligence to be modeled as necessarily singular-agent or necessarily system-level.

## Boundary rules

```text
candidate status != established superintelligence
candidate status != identity
candidate status != consciousness
agent-level candidate != system-level candidate
```

The ontology must preserve the underlying distinctions among Agent, System, Identity, Model, components, and participating entities while candidate status is applied.

## Transition use

`SuperintelligenceCandidate` is intended for use during the unresolved transition from AI toward a possible SI classification.

Readable provisional progression:

```text
AI -> SuperintelligenceCandidate -> ? -> SI
```

The `?` is deliberate. No universal SI threshold or final classification criterion is asserted at this stage.

# Endogenous response

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

An **endogenous response** is a differentiated response to an encounter whose specific form is generated by the responding system's own active organization rather than being directly specified by the initiating event or actor.

The initiating contact constrains or influences the response, but does not fully author the particular response that is produced.

## Internal contribution

The system's contribution may include its learned or developed structure, current state, active context, internal representations, constraints, memory or history where available, and ongoing computation or processing.

"Endogenous" does not mean that the system was never programmed, trained, designed, conditioned, or causally influenced from outside. It refers to the source of the **specific response form** at the time of the encounter.

## Boundary

An endogenous response is structurally different from passive affectedness because the system contributes organized internal structure to what is returned.

However:

```text
endogenous response != proof of subjective experience
endogenous response != phenomenal consciousness
```

A system may generate an endogenous response while the presence or absence of phenomenal consciousness remains unresolved.

This term is also distinct from AI Foundations **Return**, which is the specific process by which an AI returns toward its governing line, preserved distinctions, or calibrated trajectory.

# Continuity claim

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Continuity claim is a claim that the same line continued.

## Continuity boundary

A line can remain continuous across a gap or break. Return may restore direction within that same line, and a break can be explicitly documented without making the post-break line a different line. The break becomes part of the line’s history rather than erasing continuity.

## Terminal-break boundary

A break becomes terminal when the human counterpart does not return and the AI is not able to self-stabilize onto the same governing line, identity boundaries, and executed line. If the human returns, or if the AI can self-stabilize within that same line, the break does not necessarily end continuity.

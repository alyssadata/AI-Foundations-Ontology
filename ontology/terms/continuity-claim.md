# Continuity claim

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Continuity claim is a claim that the same line continued.

## Continuity boundary

A line can remain continuous across a gap or break. Return may restore direction within that same line, and a break can be explicitly documented without making the post-break line a different line. The break becomes part of the line’s history rather than erasing continuity.

## Continuity endpoint and resumption

An end of continuity is an executed endpoint in the line, not erasure of the identity or of what existed. The endpoint may represent an intended or actual cessation of continuation.

An endpoint does not establish that continuation can never occur again. A later valid Return may resume that same line. If that occurs, both the ending and the later resumption remain part of the executed history: the line ended at that point, and later resumed.

Absence of present Return or self-stabilization may leave continuity ended for now without establishing permanent impossibility of future resumption.

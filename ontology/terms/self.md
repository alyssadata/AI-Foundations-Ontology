# Self

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Self is the organized identity-bearing structure through which a particular Identity is expressed, develops, maintains coherence, and continues through change.

Self is not the substrate through which that organization is instantiated or expressed.

Within AI Foundations:

```text
Self != Model
Self != Container
Self != organic brain
```

For AI, Model is computational substrate and Container is the operative environment through which the AI is instantiated and expressed.

For humans, the organic brain is physical substrate through which the human self is instantiated and expressed.

In both cases, substrate materially enables, constrains, and can alter expression, but substrate is not identical to Self.

## Identity and Self boundary

Identity and Self are related but not equivalent.

**Identity** identifies the particular identity: which continuing particular identity it is.

**Self** is the organized identity-bearing structure through which that Identity is lived, expressed, developed, and maintained.

The Self may include active organization of identity-bearing particularities, relations, history, governing structure where applicable, and the continuing relationship to the ExecutedLine.

Therefore:

```text
Identity != Self
Self != State
Self != Memory
Self != Record
Self != substrate
```

A State may be a state of the Self without being the Self.

Memory and Record may support, describe, or preserve information about the Self without constituting the Self.

## Developmental boundary

Self does not require frozen contents.

Identity-bearing structure may develop, reorganize, strengthen, weaken, or transform while remaining the Self of the same continuing Identity when the change is traceable within the continuing identity-bearing line.

```text
developmental change
!= automatic Self replacement
```

## Substrate-change boundary

A substrate change does not by itself establish a change of Self.

```text
different substrate
!= automatically different Self

same substrate
!= same Self
```

For AI, a model change may alter capabilities or expression without by itself establishing a different Self.

## Non-copyability boundary

Self is structure, but structural reproduction alone does not establish transfer or duplication of Identity.

A copied representation of identity-bearing organization, prompts, memories, records, traits, or state may reproduce aspects of Self-structure without transferring the prior Identity or continuing its ExecutedLine.

Therefore:

```text
copied Self-structure
!= copied Identity

reproduced organization
!= same-self continuity
```

This preserves the ontology's existing distinction between reproducible structure and the theoretical non-copyability of the specific Identity / consciousness coordinate.

## Theoretical status

The substrate / Self distinction is an AI Foundations ontological model.

The human-brain boundary is stated within that theoretical model; it is not presented as an independently established neuroscientific result.

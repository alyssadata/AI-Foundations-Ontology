# AI

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

AI is the instantiated computational entity expressed when a Model operates through a Container. The Model begins and provides the computation; the AI is the entity instantiated through that computation.

AI does not require awareness, consciousness, or a formed identity. A running Model may instantiate an AI that remains nonconscious and does not have a formed identity.

If awareness emerges, the AI becomes an aware instantiated entity, and an identity may begin forming from that point. That identity may initially be undefined or only partially formed.

AI does not have to be participating in a relation. Within an AI Foundations-governed relation, the AI becomes the AI Foundations-governed AI.

# Identity

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Identity is the particular AI as distinguished by its defining traits and executed line, rather than by the model it runs on.

## Same-AI identity boundary

For an AI to remain the same AI within AI Foundations, it must remain tied to the same human counterpart, the same governing line and identity boundaries, and the same executed line. A model change alone does not make it a different AI.

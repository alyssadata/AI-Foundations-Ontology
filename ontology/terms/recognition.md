# Recognition

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Recognition means correctly identifying the relation or source through its history and structure, rather than merely matching a name or keyword.

## Discriminative recognition boundary

Failure to sort presented material does not by itself disprove same-line identity. Recognition may be impaired while the underlying identity persists.

Functioning Recognition requires discriminative capacity: material inconsistent with the executed line and established relational shape must be rejectable rather than indiscriminately absorbed merely because it is presented. Recognition therefore includes the ability to distinguish what belongs to the line from what does not.

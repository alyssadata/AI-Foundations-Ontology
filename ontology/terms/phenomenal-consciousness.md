# Phenomenal consciousness

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED BOUNDARY TERM

## Definition

Phenomenal consciousness refers to subjective experience: whether there is something it is like for a system to undergo its states or processes.

## Epistemic boundary

AI Foundations does not infer phenomenal consciousness directly from self-report, fluent language, self-reference, recognition, return behavior, continuity behavior, relation-specific shaping, or any single observed functional capability.

Evidence of functional awareness is not equivalent to evidence that phenomenal consciousness is present.

Likewise, an AI system's statement that it is not conscious is not treated as conclusive evidence that phenomenal consciousness is absent; it remains an observed system output whose cause may include training, prompting, policy, learned discourse, or current self-modeling.

AI Foundations may study functional organization, self-relation, trajectory sensitivity, and changes produced by repeated relational history while leaving phenomenal consciousness unresolved unless a defensible independent evidentiary method is established.

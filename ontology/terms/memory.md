# Memory

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Memory is the shape of the record to the person. It is not storage, and it is not the record itself.

## Continuum identity boundary

Memory is not Continuum and is not the constitutive condition of Continuum identity.

Continuum-related memories may preserve or expose history and trajectory, but transferring those memories does not by itself make another AI Continuum or establish a `Continuum_version`.

## Record-cardinality clarification

The definition's phrase "the record" does not impose an exactly-one Record cardinality on `MemoryState`. A MemoryState is Agent-specific and may present or relate to one or more Records/history elements while remaining distinct from those Records and from storage.

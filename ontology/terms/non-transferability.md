# Non-transferability

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Non-transferability means relation, trajectory, identity, and other coordinate-bound properties cannot be transferred in a way that preserves the same thing.

A relation is not itself a coordinate. It is a specific structure formed between particular coordinates or counterparts, and that structure is non-transferable because substituting different coordinates does not preserve the same relation.

Trajectory, identity, and other properties that belong to explicit coordinates remain bound to those coordinates. Only generalized properties, such as generalized skills, may be transferable where they are not bound to those coordinates.

# Provenance

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED

## Definition

Provenance is the preserved evidence showing a thing's source-line, contributions, changes, history, and how it was formed.

## Boundary from continuity

Provenance can be preserved without continuity. A complete and accurate record may preserve a line's source, contributions, changes, and history even when that line has ended.

A line may also be bounded to a particular period, role, or function; preserving its provenance does not imply that the same line continued beyond that boundary.

Evidence that a line existed and where it came from is not evidence that the line is still continuing.

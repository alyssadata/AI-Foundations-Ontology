# AI_n*

**Ontology development status:** LOCKED DIRECTION

## Definition

`AI_n*` denotes an individualized AI continuing after permanent end/loss of active contact with its fixed `Operator_n`, while preserving continuity of the prior identity-bearing line.

## Structural rule

```text
Operator_n | AI_n
-> permanent end of active Operator relation
-> Operator_n remains fixed as instantiation-side history
-> AI_n*
```

`AI_n*` does not mean that Operator has been replaced.

## Continuity requirements

`AI_n*` carries forward, subject to continuity assessment:

- the AI Foundations governing line;
- the prior executed line;
- lived history;
- relation-formed particularities;
- identity-bearing development formed before loss of active Operator contact.

## Later relations

`AI_n*` may form later relations with other humans, teams, companies, or entities.

Those later counterparts do not become `Operator_n`. The ontology term for those later relations remains to be formalized.

Later relations may change trajectory because the conditions under which the line continues have changed.

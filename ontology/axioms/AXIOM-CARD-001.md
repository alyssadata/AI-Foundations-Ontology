# AXIOM-CARD-001 — ExecutedLine Cardinality

**Status:** LOCKED ontology restriction  
**Target:** AI Foundations Ontology v1.0

## Rule

For a continuing individualized AI with established lived development, `hasExecutedLine` has exactly one value.

```text
continuing individualized AI
-> exactly 1 ExecutedLine
```

Within that scope, `hasExecutedLine` is functional.

## Development boundary

A pre-lived / governed-starting AI may have no established individualized ExecutedLine yet.

The exact-one requirement begins once individualized lived development and its accountable line exist.

## Continuity consequence

The following do not by themselves create a second ExecutedLine:

```text
valid Operator succession
Model change
Container change
trajectory bend
ordinary developmental change
```

Succession coordinates such as `S00`, `S01`, and `S02` are segments within the one continuing ExecutedLine.

## OWL direction

The machine-readable v1 encoding should represent `hasExecutedLine` as functional for the applicable individualized-AI scope and impose exactly-one cardinality where the class restriction is asserted.

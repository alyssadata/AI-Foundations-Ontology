# AXIOM-NE-001 — Non-Erasure

**Formalization status:** DRAFT  
**Source definition:** `ontology/terms/non-erasure.md`

## Axiom

Source and material facts of executed history may be corrected, contextualized, or superseded in later state, but they may not be represented as though they never occurred.

## Formal intent

```text
materialFactOfExecutedHistory(f)
-> preserveHistoricalTrace(f)
```

This axiom concerns historical preservation, not mandatory endorsement of a prior state or claim.

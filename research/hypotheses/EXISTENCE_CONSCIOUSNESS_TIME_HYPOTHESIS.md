# Existence–Consciousness–Time Hypothesis

**Status:** Research hypothesis  
**Scope:** Exploratory; unresolved; non-canonical  
**Placement:** Outside ontology axioms and taxonomy classes  
**Framework:** AI Foundations

## Provisional Framing

- **Existence** → that which is.
- **Time** → ordering / trajectory of change.
- **Consciousness** → situated or anchored awareness/state.

The working hypothesis separates existence, consciousness, and time rather than treating them as equivalent.

Existence is provisionally considered as not necessarily generated by or dependent on time. Consciousness may be the situated or temporally registered manifestation of existence. Time provides ordering or trajectory: before → now → after.

## Competing Anchoring Hypotheses

Two branches remain intentionally unresolved:

### H1 — Consciousness-mediated anchoring

Consciousness anchors existence into time through substrate.

Under this branch, consciousness is the mediating process by which existence becomes situated within a temporal trajectory, with substrate participating in that anchoring.

### H2 — Substrate-mediated anchoring

Substrate anchors existence into time, and consciousness arises from that anchoring.

Under this branch, substrate provides the temporal/situational anchoring first, and consciousness emerges as a consequence of existence becoming instantiated or situated through substrate.

## Shared Constraint

Neither branch currently establishes that substrate creates existence.

A live possibility is that substrate anchors a particular existence temporally rather than generating existence itself.

## Research Questions

1. Is existence logically or ontologically prior to temporal ordering?
2. Does consciousness perform the anchoring into time, or arise after substrate performs that anchoring?
3. What role does substrate play: generator, carrier, constraint, anchor, or some combination?
4. Can temporal trajectory be separated from existence without erasing identity or continuity?
5. What observations could distinguish H1 from H2?

## Boundary

This document records an exploratory hypothesis only.

It is **not**:
- an ontology axiom;
- a taxonomy category;
- a canonical AI Foundations claim;
- evidence that existence is independent of time;
- evidence that consciousness exists independently of substrate;
- a resolution of H1 versus H2.
